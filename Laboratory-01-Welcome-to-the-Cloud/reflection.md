# Reflection

## Laboratory 01 Reflection

This laboratory helped me understand how a cloud-based Linux environment can be accessed and investigated without relying on a local physical server.

Using commands such as `cat /etc/os-release`, `uname -r`, `lscpu`, `free -h`, and `df -h` gave me a better understanding of the operating system and the computing resources available in the environment.

I also learned the importance of documenting technical activities and keeping screenshots as evidence of completed tasks. This experience gave me a basic foundation for the succeeding cloud-computing laboratories.
